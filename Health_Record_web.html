One-paragraph synopsis

A man with prior OAGB bariatric surgery (2017) developed fatigue, low libido and a "feverish" feeling in late 2024. In Feb 2025 he was found to have severe iron-deficiency anaemia (Hb ~10, MCV 67, ferritin 4.55), vitamin D insufficiency, high LDL (189), and serology positive for enteric (typhoid) and rickettsial fever. He was admitted on 14–15 Feb 2025 and treated with ceftriaxone, doxycycline and then levofloxacin. That course was followed by antibiotic-associated (pseudomembranous) colitis, treated with vancomycin. Endoscopy on 4 Mar 2025 showed erosions at the gastro-enterostomy stoma. Colonoscopy on 20 Mar 2025 found a 15 mm splenic-flexure lesion/polyp, removed by hot biopsy; histology showed non-specific chronic inflammation, no malignancy. Stool H. pylori antigen and occult blood were negative at that time. IV iron (ferric carboxymaltose/Maltofer) fully corrected the anaemia (Hb 13.8–15.1, ferritin 107–239) from Apr 2025 to Mar 2026, and inclisiran brought LDL down to 56–78. In Jun 2026 endoscopy showed a chronic ulcer at the bypass stoma, pangastritis, and H. pylori positive (RUT +++); ultrasound showed grade-I fatty liver and new gallstones. On 8 Sep 2026 he had a presyncopal fall with head trauma. He was found to have melaena with an acute drop in Hb to 8.2, from a chronic ulcer just below the anastomosis (H. pylori RUT ++), plus bile reflux and a hiatus hernia (Hill grade IV). Brain MRI showed mild atrophy and grade-I microvascular change. EEG, echo and carotid Doppler were normal. He was treated with esomeprazole, sucralfate, tranexamic acid, and an H. pylori regimen (amoxicillin-clavulanate + levofloxacin). By 13 Oct 2026 Hb had recovered to 12.1, but ferritin was low again on 29 Sep 2026 (11.2): iron deficiency has recurred. The Jun 2026 H. pylori treatment was changed by the patient, on a doctor friend’s advice, to vonoprazan + high-dose amoxicillin (dual therapy). It did not clear the infection: the RUT was still positive on 9 Sep 2026. On 10 Sep 2026 two gastroenterologists each prescribed a different levofloxacin-based regimen. He had asked about bismuth quadruple or rifabutin-based therapy instead. He took the second one: dexlansoprazole + amoxicillin 1 g BD + levofloxacin 500 mg OD for 14 days, and is still on dexlansoprazole 30 mg BD and sucralfate suspension 1 tsp 6-hourly. Between Sep 2025 and Jun 2026 he used tirzepatide for weight loss (down to 69 kg; 72 kg now).
