## One-paragraph synopsis

A man with prior OAGB bariatric surgery (2017) developed fatigue, low libido and a "feverish" feeling in late 2024. In Feb 2025 he was found to have **severe iron-deficiency anaemia** (Hb ~10, MCV 67, ferritin 4.55), vitamin D insufficiency, high LDL (189), and serology positive for **enteric (typhoid) and rickettsial fever**. He was admitted on 14–15 Feb 2025 and treated with ceftriaxone, doxycycline and then levofloxacin. That course was followed by **antibiotic-associated (pseudomembranous) colitis**, treated with vancomycin. Endoscopy on 4 Mar 2025 showed **erosions at the gastro-enterostomy stoma**. Colonoscopy on 20 Mar 2025 found a 15 mm splenic-flexure lesion/polyp, removed by hot biopsy; histology showed **non-specific chronic inflammation, no malignancy**. Stool H. pylori antigen and occult blood were negative at that time. **IV iron (ferric carboxymaltose/Maltofer)** fully corrected the anaemia (Hb 13.8–15.1, ferritin 107–239) from Apr 2025 to Mar 2026, and inclisiran brought LDL down to 56–78. In **Jun 2026** endoscopy showed a **chronic ulcer at the bypass stoma, pangastritis, and H. pylori positive (RUT +++)**; ultrasound showed grade-I fatty liver and new **gallstones**. On **8 Sep 2026** he had a **presyncopal fall with head trauma**. He was found to have **melaena with an acute drop in Hb to 8.2**, from a **chronic ulcer just below the anastomosis (H. pylori RUT ++)**, plus **bile reflux** and a **hiatus hernia (Hill grade IV)**. Brain MRI showed mild atrophy and grade-I microvascular change. EEG, echo and carotid Doppler were normal. He was treated with esomeprazole, sucralfate, tranexamic acid, and an H. pylori regimen (amoxicillin-clavulanate + levofloxacin). By **13 Oct 2026** Hb had recovered to 12.1, but ferritin was low again on 29 Sep 2026 (11.2): **iron deficiency has recurred**.
